Workshop

Our workshop

We invest in the right tools so we find the fault the first time and fix it properly. A good diagnosis is cheaper than replacing parts at random.

  • Dealer-level diagnostic equipment
  • Qualified, experienced mechanics
  • Three bays with lifts
  • Work to manufacturer specification

Why most jobs are quoted after diagnosis

The same fault code can have several different causes, and parts prices depend on the make, the model and the engine. So we check first, explain what is actually wrong, and only then give a fixed price. No guesswork and no surprises on the invoice.
